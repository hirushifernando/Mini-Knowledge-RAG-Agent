Natural language processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence that helps computers process, understand, and generate human language.

Common NLP tasks include text classification, sentiment analysis, translation, question answering, and text summarization.

Text preprocessing can include tokenization, removing unnecessary characters, and converting text into numerical representations.

Embeddings represent words, sentences, or documents as numerical vectors. Similar meanings can have similar vector representations.

Transformer models such as BERT and GPT are widely used in modern NLP applications.
